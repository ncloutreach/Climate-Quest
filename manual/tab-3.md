The game begins with an image that depicts a climate change scenario – for example, melting Arctic ice or endangered polar bears. This image is broken into jigsaw puzzle pieces, which the player must arrange correctly on the board. 

There are three levels in this game: Easy, Medium and Difficult. Depending on the level. Either for one/two/three correct placement of puzzle piece, a quiz question will be popped up, requiring the player to answer the question to go further ahead in the game. On wrong answer, the player is not allowed to proceed further in the game till correct answer is selected. This ensures accuracy and reinforces attentiont to detail. 

As the puzzle progresses, the player not only reconstructs the image but also learns the scientific background behind it. After the entire puzzle is completed, a background story is displayed in text form. This story provides context – such as the history of Arctic climate stability, the impact of the industrial revolution and the consequences of rising greenhouse gases – giving players a comprehensive view of how human actions have transformed the environment. 

The combination of visual puzzles, quiz-based interaction and storytelling makes the game both educational and immersive, ensuring children remember not only the image but also the message behind it.
